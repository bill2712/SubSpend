Frequently asked questions

Is SubSpend free?

Yes. The tracker is free to use, with no sign-up and no paid tier. The site is supported by advertising and by optional “Buy me a coffee” contributions.

Can you see my subscriptions?

No. Everything you enter is saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. SubSpend has no server that receives your data, no accounts and no bank connections, so we never see what you track.

What happens if I clear my browser data or switch devices?

Your list is stored only in the browser where you created it. Clearing site data, using private browsing or switching to another device or browser means you will not see it there.

To move or protect your data, open “Reminders & data” and export a JSON backup. You can import that file on another device or browser at any time.

Why do I have to type the price myself?

Subscription prices depend on your country, plan and any promotion, and they change often. Rather than show a price that might be wrong for you, the catalog contains service names and categories only. The price on your own bill is the most accurate number for your budget.

How are monthly and yearly totals calculated?

Each subscription is converted to a monthly equivalent: quarterly prices are divided by 3, yearly prices by 12, and weekly prices are multiplied by about 52.18 (the average number of weeks in a year) and divided by 12. The yearly total is the monthly total multiplied by 12. Only active subscriptions are included, and shared subscriptions count only your share.

How do reminders work?

The most reliable option is the calendar file. In “Reminders & data”, download an .ics file and open it in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Each upcoming payment and trial end becomes an all-day event with an alert, so reminders work even when this site is closed. Download a fresh file after you change your subscriptions.

You can also turn on browser notifications. Because there is no server, they only appear while the site is open in a browser that allows notifications.

Where do exchange rates come from?

If you use more than one currency, the tracker downloads daily reference rates from a free public exchange-rate service, caches them for 24 hours and shows the date they were published. You can set manual rates in Settings to override them or to work offline. Reference rates differ from the rates and fees your bank applies, so converted totals are estimates.

How does splitting a shared subscription work?

When you add or edit a subscription, open “More details”, tick “Shared with others” and enter how many people share it, including you. Choose an equal split, your percentage, or a fixed amount. Totals and reminders then use your share, while the list still shows the full price.

How is “Money saved” calculated?

When you mark a subscription as cancelled, we count the payments that would have been charged after the cancellation date, up to today, and multiply them by what you paid per payment. A yearly plan cancelled a month ago shows no savings until its renewal date would have passed.

Should I enter my card number?

No — never enter a full card number. The payment method field is just a label to help you remember, such as “Visa ending 1234”. The form blocks anything that looks like a full card number.

Does SubSpend connect to my bank or cancel subscriptions for me?

No. SubSpend does not connect to banks, cards or any service, and it cannot cancel anything on your behalf. It helps you see what you pay and remember dates. To cancel, follow the provider’s own steps — our guide on cancelling subscriptions explains the general process.

Is this financial advice?

No. SubSpend and its guides provide general information only. They are not financial, legal or tax advice. For decisions about your own circumstances, consider speaking to a qualified professional.

How do I delete all my data?

Open “Reminders & data” and choose “Clear all data”. After you confirm, every subscription and setting stored by SubSpend in this browser is deleted. You can also clear this site’s data from your browser settings.
