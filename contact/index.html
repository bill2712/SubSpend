Contact

We read every message. Here is how to reach us.

Email

Write to hello@example.com. We aim to reply within a few working days.

What to include

  • Bug reports: what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and your browser and device.
  • Corrections to a guide: the page, the sentence, and a link to an official source if you have one.
  • Catalog suggestions: the service name and category. We add names only — never prices.

Please do not send your data

SubSpend never needs your subscription list to help you, and we cannot access it. Please do not email us card numbers, passwords or backup files containing personal information.

Help with a specific subscription

We are not affiliated with any listed service and cannot cancel, refund or change a subscription for you. For billing problems, contact the provider or the app store that bills you. Our guide on cancelling subscriptions explains the general steps.
